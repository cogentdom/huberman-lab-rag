Here are the primary benefits of “omega-3” fatty acids (EPA, DHA and their precursor ALA), plus four independent ways to obtain them—each with its own efficacy profile.  

1. Benefits of Omega-3s  
  • Cardiovascular Health  
    – Lowers triglycerides, modestly reduces blood pressure, improves endothelial function.  
  • Brain & Mood  
    – DHA is structural in neuronal membranes; supports neuroplasticity, cognition, memory.  
    – EPA modulates inflammation in the brain and can ease depression/anxiety.  
  • Anti-Inflammatory & Joint Health  
    – EPA/DHA inhibit pro-inflammatory cytokines, easing arthritis and muscle soreness.  
  • Eye & Prenatal Development  
    – DHA is concentrated in the retina; critical for fetal brain and visual development.  
  • Metabolic & Immune Function  
    – Improves insulin sensitivity, supports healthy immune responses.  

2. Four Supplementation Approaches  

Approach A: Plant-based ALA (e.g. flax, chia, walnuts)  
  • Pros: Widely available, rich in fiber and micronutrients.  
  • Cons: ≤10% converts to EPA/DHA; modest clinical effects on inflammation or cognition.  

Approach B: Fish-Oil (EPA+DHA) Capsules  
  • Pros: Gold-standard; dozens of RCTs showing robust triglyceride lowering, anti-inflammatory and mood benefits.  
  • Cons: Potential marine contaminants (choose third-party tested), may cause “fishy” aftertaste.  

Approach C: Krill Oil  
  • Pros: Phospholipid-bound EPA/DHA—up to 30% greater absorption than fish oil; contains antioxidant astaxanthin.  
  • Cons: Generally more expensive; lower total EPA+DHA per capsule.  

Approach D: Algal Oil (vegan EPA/DHA)  
  • Pros: Direct source of DHA (and often EPA), free of ocean-borne pollutants; ideal for vegetarians.  
  • Cons: Cost per mg of EPA/DHA is higher; fewer long-term RCTs than fish oil.  

3. Relative Effectiveness Summary  
  – Highest EPA+DHA yield per dollar: standard fish-oil concentrates.  
  – Best absorption (+ antioxidant benefit): krill oil.  
  – Pure DHA/EPA, contaminant-free: algal oil.  
  – Good for diet diversity but low potency: plant ALA.  

4. Dosage & Timing  
  – Aim for 1–3 g combined EPA+DHA daily (higher doses under medical supervision for triglyceride lowering).  
  – Take with a fat-containing meal to enhance absorption.  

Becoming “a scientist of yourself” means tracking how you feel—energy, mood, inflammation—and adjusting source and dose accordingly.
